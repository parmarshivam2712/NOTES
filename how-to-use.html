How to Use Online Sticky Notes

Mastering your browser-based notepad is easy. Follow this simple guide to create, organize, and manage your digital sticky notes.

1. Create a Note

Click the New Note button in the bottom-right dock. A fresh sticky note will instantly appear on your screen, ready for your thoughts.

2. Drag and Resize

Click and hold the top bar of any note to drag it anywhere on your screen. Use the bottom-right corner of the note to resize it for longer texts.

3. Customize Colors

Use the settings icon on the note to change its color theme. Color-coding helps you organize tasks by priority or category.

Saving and Returning

Your notes are automatically saved to your browser's local storage as you type. There is no save button! When you close the tab and return later, your notes will be exactly where you left them.
